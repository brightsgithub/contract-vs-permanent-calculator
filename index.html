Contract vs Permanent

UK comparison calibrated to your PayStream £600/day payslips.

Contract

Dynamic year-end tax estimate. The calculator estimates PAYE withheld from the tax code and compares it with final annual Income Tax. A positive balance means an estimated balancing payment; a negative balance means an estimated refund. This is cash-flow information only and is not deducted again from annual take-home.
Calibration: a £3,000 five-day week produced £2,588.66 taxable gross and £1,704.43 net on your recent non-pension payslip.

Assumptions

The umbrella model is calibrated from a £600/day PayStream reconciliation. For different day rates it models employer NI at 15% above a weekly secondary threshold of £96 and the Apprenticeship Levy at 0.5% of taxable gross. It assumes no salary-sacrifice pension on the contract side. The 2026/27 England/Wales/Northern Ireland tax model uses the £12,570 standard Personal Allowance, the £100,000 Personal Allowance taper, 20% / 40% / 45% Income Tax bands, and standard employee NI rates. The year-end tax position compares estimated final annual Income Tax with estimated PAYE withheld using the entered tax-code allowance. It supports ordinary positive numeric L-type codes such as 1257L. Special/K/BR/D0/D1/NT codes, Scottish rates, taxable benefits, untaxed income and in-year code changes require more detailed payroll modelling. Real PAYE is cumulative and payroll rounding/timing can create differences. The estimated balancing payment or refund is cash-flow information only and is not deducted again from annual take-home. This is an estimate, not tax advice.
