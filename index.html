Assumptions

The umbrella model is calibrated from your actual £600/day PayStream reconciliation: £3,000 umbrella income, £24.50 weekly margin, £373.90 employer NI, £12.94 apprenticeship levy and £2,588.66 taxable employment income. For different day rates it models employer NI at 15% above a weekly secondary threshold of £96 and levy at 0.5% of taxable gross. It assumes no salary-sacrifice pension on the contract side. PAYE estimates use a simplified annual calculation and may differ slightly from payroll because payroll is cumulative/weekly and rounding applies. Your £5,133.25 January 2027 Self Assessment balancing payment is treated as a cash-flow settlement of 2025/26 tax already represented by the annual income-tax calculation, so it is not deducted a second time from contract take-home. This is a comparison tool, not tax advice.
